# Assigning status

Read the checklist line as a question, then ask whether the documents in the data room let you *answer* it. Not whether something related exists.

## The two values that appear in the workbook

**Received** — a document exists whose evident purpose is that line, and (for material items) you have opened it and confirmed it delivers. One-to-one, no interpretation needed.

**Partial** — something responsive exists, but a specific, nameable piece of the ask is not covered. The note must say which piece.

**Blank** — everything else. Not received, not applicable, buyer-side, process-only, or simply not yet requested. A blank cell is an open row that fills in as documents arrive. Do not write "Not found" or "N/A" into the status column.

Internally you may track richer states while working (Not received, Disputed, N/A, Review required) — but collapse them before writing the workbook. In particular: **a disputed item is written as Partial**, with the dispute explained in the note.

## The four shapes Partial takes

1. **Multi-part ask, partly answered.** "Staff schedule; names; job descriptions; annual compensation; benefits; existing policies" — census, job descriptions and handbook posted; benefits not. → *"Waiting on benefits"*
2. **A sample instead of the population.** A folder literally named *Sample* Vendor Agreements. Seven top customer contracts out of 120 accounts. → say which subset arrived.
3. **Source documents exist, the requested synthesis does not.** "Summary of special customer rights" — the rights live inside individual amendments; nobody has abstracted them. The facts are in the room; the deliverable is not.
4. **Actuals without the forward view.** Utility bills but no utility budget. Historical capex but no 5-year plan. Tax bills but no re-assessment estimate.

## Things that look like gaps but are not

- **Buyer-side workstreams.** Debt financing, deposits, buyer authority documents, JV docs. Leave blank; the seller was never going to post these.
- **Process items.** Interviews, government meetings, contractor selection. Leave blank.
- **Genuinely inapplicable.** Seismic reports in Florida. Note it once in chat; leave the row blank.

## Things that look fine but are not

- **A folder that exists but is empty.** Worse than missing — it reads as delivered in any folder-level review.
- **A document that answers a *different* question than the line asks.** A permit close-out is not a certificate of occupancy. A banker's model is not a management budget. A reliance letter is not the report it refers to.
- **A summary file whose name promises more than its contents.** Open it.

## Escalating a line item

Prefer the harsher status when a material item is involved and you have not opened the document. It is cheaper to upgrade a row after reading than to have the user rely on a Received that was a guess.
